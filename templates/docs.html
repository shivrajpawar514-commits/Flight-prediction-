Endpoint Catalog & Schemas

POST /api/predict

Predicts fare, 80% & 95% confidence intervals, game-theoretic SHAP feature attributions, and Buy/Wait advice.

Params: Dep_Time, Arrival_Time, Source, Destination, stops, airline, model_name
POST /api/whatif

Executes instant sensitivity analysis across competing airlines, stop counts, and departure hour shifts.

Params: Dep_Time, Arrival_Time, Source, Destination, stops, airline
POST /api/recommend

Multi-Criteria Decision Making (TOPSIS) flight ranking and 2D Pareto Optimal trade-off set.

Params: weight_price, weight_duration, weight_reliability, weight_stops, weight_time, source, destination
GET /api/benchmark

Returns evaluation metrics ($R^2$, RMSE, MAE, MAPE, 5-Fold Cross-Val, Latency) for all 6 models.

Returns: JSON Benchmark Leaderboard & Feature Importances
