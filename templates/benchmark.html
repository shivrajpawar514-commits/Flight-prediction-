AI/ML Theoretical Principles Applied in AeroPulse AI

Fundamental Methodologies

1. Stacking Meta-Ensemble

Combines predictions of Bagged Trees (Random Forest, Extra Trees) and Gradient Boosters (XGBoost) through an L2-Regularized Ridge meta-regressor, reducing variance while maintaining low bias.

2. Explainable AI (SHAP Axioms)

Utilizes cooperative game theory (Shapley Values) satisfying Efficiency, Symmetry, Dummy Player, and Additivity axioms to allocate fair rupee-level feature attributions for every prediction.

3. 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation

Guarantees out-of-fold generalization by partitioning training sets across 5 price quintiles, preventing data leakage and quantifying standard deviation of error ($CV_{R^2} \pm \sigma$).

4. Multi-Criteria TOPSIS & Pareto

Employs vector-normalized Euclidean distance to positive and negative ideal solutions, filtering the non-dominated Pareto front in the multi-dimensional price-duration trade-off space.
